Technical review

Connections

Zepbound: three connections from one WebSDK, split by audience (Consumer 6M, HCP 345k, Lilly HCP 941k). The split has no PII justification because it is ECID-only.

Mounjaro: four connections split by geography and channel (US and Canada combined, US only, Canada only, Email/AJO). A unified consumer and HCP dataset is an advantage over Zepbound.

Data quality

linkClicks inflation is confirmed on both brands. A stale value of 1 is carried on every non-click event, which overcounts by 8 to 10 times.

Architecture

36 datasets across two sandboxes.

Cross-domain ECID is confirmed working through the lilly.com root cookie.

Findings beyond scope

  1. 1Enrollment domain is untaggedHighenrollment.zepbound.lilly.com has no WebSDK, so patient enrollment cannot be measured.
  2. 2Three Zepbound connections from one WebSDKHighOne configId with three audience-based connections, all ECID-only. The split does not serve data governance.
  3. 3Identity resolution gapHighGBS is live in one connection but targets HCPREFID, which carries winner rotation and lexicographic selection risks. WebSDK connections remain unstitched ECID-only.
  4. 4No offline conversion dataHighCJA has no datasets with offline events such as Rx write-ups or purchases, so the digital journey cannot be measured end to end.
